Collector's order allegedly drafted with AI

Medipalli Komuraiah v. State of Telangana (writ petition)

Court
Telangana High Court
Bench
N.V. Shravan Kumar J
Status
Pending
Next hearing
Listed 18 Nov 2026; next date awaited
Last updated
23 November 2026

Key issues

  1. Can a revenue order in a land dispute, allegedly passed ex parte with AI tools and without verifying facts, stand?
  2. What action does the State propose to take?

Where it stands

On 3 November Justice N.V. Shravan Kumar suspended the Jangaon Collector's order of 8 September, issued notice to the Revenue Principal Secretary, the Collector and the Tahsildar, and asked the Advocate-General what action is proposed. Listed on 18 November; the outcome has not been reported yet.
