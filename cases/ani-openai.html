AI training and copyright: ANI's appeal

ANI Media Pvt. Ltd. v. OpenAI (appeal against the order of 24 July 2026 in CS(COMM) 1028/2024)

Court
Delhi High Court (Division Bench)

Early Dec 2026 (5 or 8 Dec reported)

Key issues

  1. Is training ChatGPT on ANI's news content fair dealing for research under s. 52 of the Copyright Act?
  2. Was the single judge right to refuse ANI an interim injunction?

Where it stands

The single judge refused ANI an interim injunction on 24 July 2026. A Division Bench issued notice on ANI's appeal on 15 September and gave no interim direction. It is listed in early December; reports give 5 or 8 December.
