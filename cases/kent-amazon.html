Brand names sold as advertising keywords on Amazon

Kent RO Systems Ltd. v. Amazon Seller Services Pvt. Ltd. (CS(COMM) 1145/2026)

Court
Delhi High Court
Case number
CS(COMM) 1145/2026
Bench
Tejas Karia J
Status
Pending
Next hearing
8 December 2026
Last updated
25 November 2026

Key issues

  1. Does letting sellers bid on the well-known mark KENT as a sponsored-ads keyword infringe it?
  2. Can Amazon claim intermediary safe harbour for keyword advertising?

Where it stands

On 16 October Justice Tejas Karia issued summons on Kent's application for an ad interim injunction; Amazon accepted notice. No interim relief has been granted. Amazon's reply is due within three weeks and its written statement within 30 days.
