Key issues

  1. Must an Indian advertiser's suit over disabled ad accounts be heard in California under Meta's terms?

Where it stands

On 27 October the court refused Meta's application: claims under the IT Act and IT Rules need an Indian forum. The suit for restoration and damages continues; jurisdiction can still be argued at trial.
