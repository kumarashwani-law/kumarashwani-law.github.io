User must get a copy of a takedown notice

Haryana 24 News Network v. Union of India

Court
Punjab & Haryana High Court
Status
Decided
Decided
20 October 2026
Last updated
23 November 2026

Key issues

  1. Was an SP's s. 79(3)(b) notice that got a news page blocked valid?

Where it stands

Reported 20 October 2026: the notice was without jurisdiction (Rule 3(1)(d) needs a DIG-rank officer), a copy must go to the user, and the page was ordered restored.

Timeline

  1. 20 October 2026SP's notice held to be without jurisdiction; the page ordered restored (date reported).
