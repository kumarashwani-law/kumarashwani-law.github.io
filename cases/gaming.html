Key issues

  1. Can Parliament ban all online money games, including games of skill, under the Online Gaming Act, 2025?

Where it stands

On 5 August 2026 a bench led by the Chief Justice agreed to a final hearing. There is no interim stay, so the Act (in force since 1 May 2026) applies.

Timeline

  1. 5 August 2026The Court agrees to a final hearing; no interim stay.
  2. 1 May 2026The Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025 comes into force.
