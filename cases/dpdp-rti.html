Challenge to the DPDP Act and the RTI amendment

Venkatesh Nayak v. Union of India (W.P.(C) 177/2026) and connected petitions, including the Digital News Publishers Association

Court
Supreme Court
Case number
W.P.(C) 177/2026 (lead petition) and connected matters
Status
Pending
Next hearing
9 December 2026
Last updated
23 November 2026

Key issues

  1. Is s. 44(3) of the DPDP Act, which narrowed the RTI Act's exemption for personal information, constitutional?
  2. Are ss. 17, 33 and 36 of the Act, and the rules for selecting the Data Protection Board, valid?
  3. Does the Act need an exemption for journalism?

Where it stands

Notice issued and no interim stay. The RTI petitions were referred to a larger bench in February 2026. On 27 October the Court issued notice on the news publishers' petition and declined an interim stay without hearing the Union. The matter was listed with the connected petitions on 17 November but could not be taken up, and was adjourned to 9 December.
