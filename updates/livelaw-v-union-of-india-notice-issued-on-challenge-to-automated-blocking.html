LiveLaw v. Union of India | Notice issued on challenge to automated blocking

Ashwani KumarAdvocate · 19 November 2026 · Bench: Nitin Wasudeo Sambre ACJ, Sanjay Sharma J

On 19 November, a Delhi High Court Bench of Acting Chief Justice Nitin Wasudeo Sambre and Justice Sanjay Sharma issued notice to the Union of India and Meta on LiveLaw's petition challenging Rule 4(4) of the IT Rules, 2021, which requires large platforms to use automated tools to restrict content exactly identical to content already removed.

LiveLaw says three Instagram reels reporting Supreme Court hearings were restricted in India, two within about 15 minutes of posting, without any order, notice or reasons. The Additional Solicitor General suggested treating the petition as a representation and passing a reasoned order; LiveLaw's counsel called that a mere formality.

The Union and Meta have four weeks to file replies. Interim relief was not pressed. The matter is listed next on 18 January 2027, and similar challenges on 9 February 2027.
