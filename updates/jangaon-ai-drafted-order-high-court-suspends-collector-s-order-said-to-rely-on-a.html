Jangaon AI-drafted order | High Court suspends Collector's order said to rely on AI

Ashwani KumarAdvocate · 3 November 2026 · Bench: N.V. Shravan Kumar J

On 3 November, Justice N.V. Shravan Kumar of the Telangana High Court suspended the Jangaon Collector's order of 8 September in a land dispute. The petitioner says the order was passed ex parte, with AI tools and without verifying the facts.

The Court issued notice to the Revenue Principal Secretary, the Collector and the Tahsildar, and asked the Advocate-General what action is proposed. The matter was listed on 18 November; the outcome has not been reported yet.
