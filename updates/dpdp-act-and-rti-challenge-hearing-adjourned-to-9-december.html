DPDP Act and RTI challenge | Hearing adjourned to 9 December

Ashwani KumarAdvocate · 17 November 2026

On 17 November, the Supreme Court listed the challenge to the DPDP Act's amendment of the RTI Act together with the connected petitions, including the Digital News Publishers Association's. The matter could not be taken up and was adjourned to 9 December.

On 27 October, the Court had issued notice on the news publishers' petition and declined an interim stay without hearing the Union. The RTI petitions were referred to a larger bench in February 2026.
