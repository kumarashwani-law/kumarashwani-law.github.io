Kent RO v. Amazon | Summons issued; no interim relief yet

Ashwani KumarAdvocate · 16 October 2026 · Bench: Tejas Karia J

On 16 October, Justice Tejas Karia of the Delhi High Court issued summons in Kent RO Systems' suit against Amazon over the use of KENT as a sponsored-ads keyword, and on Kent's application for an ad interim injunction. Amazon accepted notice.

No interim relief has been granted. Amazon's reply to the application is due within three weeks and its written statement within 30 days. The application is listed next on 8 December.
