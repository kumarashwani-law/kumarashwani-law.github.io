This week in AI, tech and law (8–14 November), in one place.

Ashwani KumarAdvocate · 14 November 2026 · 2 min read
View on LinkedIn

⚖️ In the courts
• Madras HC orders a DVAC FIR and account freezes over "AI-generated" books bought for Tamil Nadu's public libraries
• AAP's deepfake posts on CM Rekha Gupta, ordered removed on 6 November, were still live on X four days later
• Supreme Court (order of 29 October, reported this week): banks should compensate digital-arrest victims where they fail in due diligence
• Delhi HC asks the Centre why a political cartoonist's Instagram account was withheld in India

🔍 Policing and surveillance
• The Home Minister approved a ₹4.98 crore Cellebrite phone-cracking tool for the NIA, with a "Make in India" waiver
• Reuters: DoT blocked 129 betting sites on forged Rajasthan and Allahabad High Court orders; police are probing
• Hyderabad Police launch "Dooradrishti", an AI tool that profiles accused persons and maps criminal networks

🔐 Data protection
• Consent manager registration under the DPDP Rules opens on 13 November, but the Data Protection Board still has no members
• MeitY questions Pronto and other home-services platforms over in-home footage used to train robots
• NCPCR plans draft social media guidelines for children, to be finalised in 2027 (report)

📱 Platforms and consumers
• CCPA fines Ola ₹10 lakh: advance tips are a "pre-service toll" and a dark pattern
• The Centre steps back from a "kill switch" for long calls, favouring WhatsApp's warnings-first plan against scams
• The government is likely to push indigenous AI models in critical sectors (report)

🌍 Around the world
• EU proposes the Digital Fairness Act: bans on manipulative design and stealth influencer ads, fines up to 6% of turnover
• Japan flags privacy risks in AI and ad practices at Google, Meta, TikTok and LINE Yahoo

📅 Dates to watch
17 Nov: Supreme Court hears the DPDP Act and RTI challenges
18 Nov: Telangana HC on the Jangaon Collector's AI-drafted order
23 Nov: Supreme Court on digital-arrest scams
30 Nov: RBI's KYC directions due
13 May 2027: most DPDP duties begin

The thread this week: powers over our phones, data and feeds are growing faster than the rules. Courts are filling the gaps one case at a time.

Which of these matters most to you? Links to sources are in the comments.
