One year ago today, India notified its data protection rules. Today, the next phase begins. But there's still no one in charge.

Ashwani KumarAdvocate · 13 November 2026
View on LinkedIn

The DPDP Rules were notified on 13 November 2025. That day, the Data Protection Board was established in law, with four Members besides a Chairperson. A year later, as of early November, none of those posts had been filled.

What starts today: consent manager registration (s. 6(9) and Rule 4). Consent managers are registered platforms through which you give, manage and withdraw consent across many businesses. To register, a company must be incorporated in India, have a net worth of at least ₹2 crore and run an independently certified platform. Applications go to the Board, which has no members to decide them.

The gap is already showing:
🔹 In April, the Madhya Pradesh High Court sent a petitioner to the Board, to be decided within 15 days. There was no one to decide it
🔹 On 7 October, the Kerala High Court recorded that the Board wasn't functional and expected it to be working within eight weeks, roughly by 2 December
🔹 The Board's selection process is itself under challenge in the Supreme Court, listed on 17 November

What starts on 13 May 2027 (slide 5):
✔ Security safeguards: penalty up to ₹250 crore
✔ Breach notice to the Board and users: up to ₹200 crore
✔ Children's data and parental consent: up to ₹200 crore
✔ Significant data fiduciary duties: up to ₹150 crore
✔ Notice, consent, erasure and user rights: up to ₹50 crore

MeitY floated shortening some timelines, but nothing has been notified. In law, 13 May 2027 stands.

My take: a law without a regulator is a law on hold. No approvals, no standards, and very little time to build capacity before complaints start arriving in May. The government should appoint independent members now, through a published process.

For businesses, six months to go (slide 7):
1️⃣ Now to February: map your data, flag children's data, add processing clauses to vendor contracts, and draft clear notices
2️⃣ March to May: make consent easy to withdraw, put in encryption, access controls and logs, and set up a 72-hour breach playbook and a grievance contact

The duties apply from May 2027 whether or not the regulator is ready.
