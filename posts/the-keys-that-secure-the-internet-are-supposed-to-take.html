The keys that secure the internet are supposed to take billions of years to guess. An AI agent guessed these in one prompt.

Ashwani KumarAdvocate · 22 October 2026 · 2 min read
View on LinkedIn

What happened:
eMudhra, a licensed Indian certificate authority (CA), published eight intermediate certificate keys in April 2025. Because of a faulty step in its key-generation ceremony, each private key was a number below 256. Trying 256 values was enough to recover them. Security researcher Scott Helme said he recovered all eight in under a second, and that an AI agent did the same from a single prompt.

The flaw went unnoticed for about 18 months, through two audit cycles. Once a researcher reported it on 17 October, Mozilla and Chrome blocked the certificates the same day, Apple and Microsoft followed, and eMudhra revoked all eight.

eMudhra says it has found no evidence of misuse, that only one US customer ecosystem was affected, and that its Indian digital signature and eSign services run on separate systems and are unaffected.

Why lawyers should care:
A CA is a digital notary. Whoever holds its private key can forge trusted certificates, impersonate websites and intercept encrypted traffic.

What the IT Act says (Chapter VI):
🔹 Section 30: a CA must use hardware, software and procedures secure from intrusion and misuse
🔹 Section 34(2): if an event affects the integrity of its systems, it must use reasonable efforts to notify those likely to be affected
🔹 Section 25: the Controller of Certifying Authorities (CCA) can suspend or revoke a licence for failing to maintain Section 30 procedures

The questions this raises (my take):
1️⃣ Two regimes: the IT Act licenses CAs for electronic signature certificates; website certificates are policed mainly by browser root programs. Do Section 30 duties reach a licensed CA's global TLS business? That's untested.
2️⃣ The weak keys passed two audits. What were the auditors checking?
3️⃣ Who compensates relying parties? Section 43A covers only sensitive personal data, and DPDP security duties start in May 2027. For now, remedies sit mostly in contracts and their liability caps.

The contrast is striking: four foreign browser makers acted within 72 hours. As of 21 October, the CCA had made no public statement, though MeitY officials said a team was investigating.
