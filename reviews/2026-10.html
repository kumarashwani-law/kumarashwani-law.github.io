In court

  1. 7 October 2026Data Protection Board (Union Government): The Kerala High Court records that the Board is not functional.
  2. 15 October 2026Children's online safety (Supreme Court): Order: internet access is a fundamental right for children too; blanket age-based bans are disproportionate. Directions on a child mode, a national hash database, faster takedowns and fines scaled to platform size.
  3. 16 October 2026Kent RO v. Amazon (Delhi High Court): Summons issued on Kent's application for an ad interim injunction; Amazon accepts notice. No interim relief granted.
  4. 20 October 2026Haryana 24 News Network (Punjab & Haryana High Court): SP's notice held to be without jurisdiction; the page ordered restored (date reported).
  5. 27 October 2026DPDP Act and RTI challenge (Supreme Court): Notice issued on the Digital News Publishers Association's petition; interim stay declined without hearing the Union.
  6. 27 October 2026Meta v. Blink Digital (Bombay High Court): The court refuses Meta's application: claims under the IT Act and IT Rules need an Indian forum.
  7. 27 October 2026Sarthak Bhagat v. Union of India (Delhi High Court): Writ held not maintainable against Meta; the GAC directed to decide the appeal quickly.
  8. 29 October 2026Digital arrest scams (Supreme Court): The Court says the RBI's new rules must make banks compensate victims where a failure of due diligence enabled the fraud, and asks WhatsApp and other intermediaries for their prevention plans.
