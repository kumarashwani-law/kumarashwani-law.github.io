Get the app

Install this website as an app on your phone or computer. It opens full screen from its own icon, keeps pages you've read available offline, and updates itself whenever new writing is published. It's free, and there's no app store involved.

The app is installed on this device. Open it from your home screen or app list.

Android phone (Chrome)

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install the app above, or tap the ⋮ menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Confirm. The AK icon appears on your home screen.

iPhone or iPad (Safari)

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Computer (Chrome or Edge)

  1. Click Install the app above, or the install icon at the right of the address bar.
  2. Confirm. The app opens in its own window and appears with your other apps.

Good to know

  • Pages you've opened before can be read without internet.
  • The app has no ads and doesn't need any permissions.
  • To remove it, uninstall it like any other app.
